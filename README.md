#SSTV TOOLKIT#
                                                                     
(by u/nutzandboltz112)

-INTRODUCTION---------------------------------------------------------------------


Hello! Thank you for downloading this program! This program is simple to use!
Has everything you need for converting images into signals and decoding the
said signals, no need for a billion conversion programs, this one got you
covered.


--INSTRUCTIONS---------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Click "Start SSTV Toolkit" to open the program
2. In "Encode" pick the image that you like
3. Chouse an SSTV mode of your liking 
4. Pick any of the photo proportion settings ('stretch to fill' recommended)
5. In the "Decode" section browse for your converted (.wav) file and insert it
6. Press "Decode"

-ADDITIONAL-INFORMATION------------------------------------------------------------

If you're seeking the glitchy look for your converted images by actually scanning
them then go to "Live Monitor".

Live Monitor uses your mic to hear the signals emitted by your .wav file to slowly
scan it into an image.

Make sure to position your mic near your speaker, press "Start Listening" to 
activate mic functions then play your audio file.

In roughly a minute you will have your photo and after completion you will also
have a button if you want to save your picture.
